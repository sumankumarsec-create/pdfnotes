How to Get More Job Interviews in Tech

A practical system for generating more interviews across full-time, freelance, remote, and contract tech work — why cold applications alone rarely work, and how to shift effort toward referrals, direct outreach, and visible proof of work instead. Covers resume keyword-matching for ATS filters, messaging hiring managers directly, role-specific tactics for corporate, freelance, and remote roles, a tracking system to iterate on your funnel, common mistakes that suppress your interview rate, and a first-week action checklist.

Why it's on this site

Short by design — four pages, because the core idea doesn't need padding: cold applications alone don't work as well as referrals and direct outreach, and most people already know this but don't act on it. The ATS keyword-matching section is the most immediately actionable part. If you want a mindset shift plus a checklist you can start on today, this is built for exactly that, not a comprehensive job-search manual.
